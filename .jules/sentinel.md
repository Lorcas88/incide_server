## 2024-06-30 - [Missing Rate Limiters on Sensitive Auth Endpoints]
**Vulnerability:** The `/register` and `/forgot-password` endpoints in `auth.routes.js` are currently missing their required rate limiters (`registerLimiter` and `forgotPasswordLimiter`).
**Learning:** These endpoints were created but lacked protection against abuse (e.g. brute forcing email addresses or mass generating emails/accounts) since rate limiting wasn't applied directly to these unauthenticated endpoints. In tests, the limiters were also completely skipped making it easier to forget their importance. The application relies on `express-rate-limit`, but state-changing endpoints without authentication need specific per-endpoint limitations beyond the global configuration.
**Prevention:** Always ensure unauthenticated endpoints (like login, register, forgot-password, reset-password, and token-refresh) have specific rate limiters applied at the route definition. Rely on integration/e2e testing that explicitly checks for HTTP 429 status codes under high load to verify the middleware configuration.
